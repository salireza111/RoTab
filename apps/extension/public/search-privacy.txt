RoTab — search privacy
Effective date: 15 October 2026
Publisher: Alireza
Contact: salireza111@gmail.com

Browser default submits searches through the browser's Search API and does not
request autocomplete. The browser's selected search provider handles the search.
Every new tab and reload starts on Browser default, which is the first option.

Selecting Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave or Bing directs submitted searches to that
provider for the current tab. Selecting a named provider also requests optional access to its
suggestions service. Once access is granted, suggestions are enabled and partial
queries of at least two characters are sent after a short pause in typing.
Search suggestions can be disabled in the engine menu. Declining access keeps
searches available without suggestions. Search settings in the browser remain
unchanged.

Search and suggestions services receive the query and ordinary connection
metadata, including the source IP address. Suggestions are requested directly
from the selected provider over HTTPS, without cookies or a referrer. The
selected provider applies its own privacy practices. Requests are not sent to
another provider if the selected service is unavailable.

The selected engine is held in the current tab's memory. It is not saved as an
opening default or synchronized with other tabs. RoTab saves the suggestions
enabled/disabled preference in the local browser profile. Query text and
suggestions are held only in the current page's memory;
RoTab does not retain a search history, send it to the publisher, or share it
with volunteer peers. Browser navigation history and external providers' logs
are controlled separately by the browser and those services.
